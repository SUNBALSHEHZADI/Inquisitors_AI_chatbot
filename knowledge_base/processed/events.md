# Inquisitors Society — Events and Activities

## Overview

Inquisitors Society participates in educational, intellectual, competitive,
and student-focused activities.

Activities may include events, competitions, workshops, seminars,
learning sessions, and collaborative programs.

## Publicly Documented Event

### Living the Sunnah ﷺ Exhibition

The University of Engineering and Technology Lahore published an official
announcement for an exhibition organized by Al-Madinah Islamic Research
Center in collaboration with Inquisitors Quiz Society.

The event included:

- Poster gallery
- Seerah models
- Virtual Reality experience
- Interactive 3D views
- Scholar-led explanations
- Quiz activities
- Certificates

The event was held at the Auditorium Complex, UET Lahore, on
1 and 2 October 2025.

## Competitions

Publicly available references also show participation in quiz and
student competition activities associated with Inquisitors.

## Event Information

For every new event, the chatbot should preferably retrieve:

- Event title
- Event type
- Date
- Time
- Venue
- Organizer
- Registration information
- Eligibility
- Speaker or guest information
- Certificate information
- Official announcement link

## Current Event Information

The chatbot must not invent event dates, venues, speakers, registration
links, or eligibility requirements.

If current information is unavailable in the knowledge base, the chatbot
should direct the user to the official communication channels.

> NOTE: Historical event information should not automatically be treated
> as an upcoming event.
