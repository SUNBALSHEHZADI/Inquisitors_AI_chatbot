# Inquisitors Society — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Inquisitors Society?

Inquisitors Society is a student-focused learning community
that provides opportunities for learning, collaboration,
skill development, internships, training, and events.

## What internship domains are available?

Example domains include AI, Machine Learning, Data Science,
Research, Web Development, Content Writing, and Graphic Design.

## How can I become a member?

Students can follow the official membership registration
procedure provided by the administration.

## Does Inquisitors organize workshops?

Yes. The society may organize workshops, seminars, training
sessions, and competitions.

## Where can I find official announcements?

Students should follow the official communication channels
of Inquisitors Society for verified announcements.

## What should I do if the chatbot cannot answer my question?

The chatbot should provide a fallback response and direct
the user toward the official Inquisitors support/contact
channel.
