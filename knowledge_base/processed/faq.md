# Inquisitors Society — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Inquisitors Society?

Inquisitors Society is a student-focused learning community
that provides opportunities for learning, collaboration,
skill development, internships, training, and events.

## What internship domains are available?

Example domains include AI, Machine Learning, Data Science,
Research, Web Development, Content Writing, and Graphic Design.
