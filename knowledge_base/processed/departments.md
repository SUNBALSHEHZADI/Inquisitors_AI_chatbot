# Inquisitors Society — Departments

## Overview

Inquisitors Society may organize its activities across different
technical, creative, research, and community-related areas.

Departments or domains can help students participate according to their
interests and skills.

## Example Domains

The prototype knowledge base includes the following example areas:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Data Science
- Research
- Web Development
- Content Writing
- Graphic Design
- Digital Marketing
- Community and Management

## Department Activities

Depending on the area, students may participate in activities such as:

- Learning and training sessions
- Practical assignments
- Projects
- Research activities
- Workshops
- Competitions
- Team collaboration
- Community activities
- Skill development

The exact responsibilities and activities of each department should be
confirmed through official information provided by the administration.

## Domain Selection

Students may choose or be assigned to an area according to the
available opportunities and the structure of the relevant program.

Specific selection criteria, assignments, leadership roles, or
departmental responsibilities should not be assumed unless officially
provided.

## Important Information Policy

The chatbot must not invent:

- Department heads
- Department-specific rules
- Selection criteria
- Team structures
- Schedules
- Responsibilities
- Application procedures

If specific departmental information is unavailable, the chatbot
should state that the information is currently unavailable and direct
the student to the official Inquisitors Society communication channels.

> Note: The department/domain list in this prototype is demonstration
> data and should be replaced or refined when official administrative
> information is provided.
