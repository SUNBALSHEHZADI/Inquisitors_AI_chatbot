## Welcome to Inquisitors AI Assistant!

The Inquisitors AI Assistant is here to help you find information about the Inquisitors Society quickly and easily. This guide will show you how to use the chatbot effectively.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Opening the Chat

1. Click the **"Ask Inquisitors AI"** button on the website
2. A chat window will open in the bottom-right corner
3. You'll see a message area and an input field
4. Start typing your question!

### Your First Question

Try asking something like:
- "What is Inquisitors Society?"
- "How can I become a member?"
- "What internships are available?"
- "How do I contact Inquisitors?"

---

## 💬 How to Ask Questions

### Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Ask clear, specific questions
- Use natural language
- Spell approximately correctly
- Ask one main topic at a time
- Ask follow-up questions

❌ **DON'T:**
- Ask about unrelated topics
- Type in ALL CAPS
- Ask multiple complex questions at once
- Be discouraged if first answer isn't perfect

### Question Examples

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| General | "What is Inquisitors Society?" |
| Specific | "Tell me about the AI internship" |
| Process | "How do I apply for internship?" |
| Contact | "What's the phone number?" |
| Events | "When is the next competition?" |
| Membership | "What are membership benefits?" |

---

## 📝 Understanding Responses

### Response Format

Each response includes:
- **Main answer** to your question
- **Clear formatting** with bullets or steps
- **Relevant details** and context
- **Contact info** when you need to reach someone

### Good Response Example

```
User: "What internships are available?"

Bot: "Inquisitors offers internship opportunities in:
     • AI & Machine Learning
     • Data Science
     • Web Development
     • Content Writing
     • Graphic Design
     
     Internships typically last 3-6 months with 
     hands-on project experience."
```

## 🔄 Multi-Turn Conversations

### What is a Follow-up Question?

After the chatbot answers your question, you can ask related follow-ups. The chatbot will understand the context.

### Example Conversation

```
You: "What internships are available?"

Bot: "We offer internships in AI, Data Science, 
      Web Development, and more..."

You: "Tell me about the AI internship"

Bot: "The AI internship covers machine learning, 
      deep learning, and NLP projects..."

You: "How do I apply?"

Bot: "To apply: 1) Submit your CV, 
            2) Write a statement of interest,
            3) Complete an interview"
```

## ⏰ Session Management

### What is a Session?

A session is your conversation with the chatbot. It's saved automatically.

### How Sessions Work

- **First Visit:** Chatbot creates a unique session ID
- **Your Questions:** All messages are saved
- **Return Later:** Open chat again and see your history
- **Continue:** You can ask more questions anytime

### Your Information is Saved

- Conversations stored securely
- Session lasts for 30 days
- You can view history anytime
- Messages only visible to you

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What if the chatbot doesn't understand?

**A:** Try rephrasing your question:
- Break into smaller questions
- Be more specific
- Use simpler words
- Ask about related topics

### Q: What if I get an error message?

**A:** 
- Try refreshing the page
- Check your internet connection
- Rephrase your question
- Contact support if problem persists

### Q: Can I clear my conversation history?

**A:** 
- Close the chat window (history saved)
- Open again to continue
- To clear history, contact support
- Or start fresh in new browser tab

- ✅ Society information

- ✅ Events and training
- ✅ Membership details
- ✅ Contact information

- Updated regularly
- Current as of last update

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Be Specific

❌ Bad: "Tell me about stuff"  
✅ Good: "What internship domains are available?"

### Tip 2: Ask Step by Step

❌ Bad: "Tell me everything about internships, membership, events, and training"  
✅ Good: "What are the internship opportunities?"

### Tip 3: Use Follow-ups

❌ Bad: Repeat full question each time  
✅ Good: "Tell me about AI. How long is it? How do I apply?"

### Tip 4: Try Different Wording

If first answer isn't perfect:
- "Tell me more about X"
- "Can you elaborate on Y"
- "What does that mean?"
- "Give me an example"

### Tip 5: Check Contact Info

Don't know? Ask for contact:
- "How do I reach you?"
- "What's your phone number?"
- "Email for internships?"

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### Chat Won't Open

1. Refresh the page (F5)
2. Try a different browser
3. Clear browser cache
4. Check internet connection

### Chatbot Not Responding

1. Wait a moment (may be loading)
2. Try a simpler question
3. Refresh the page
4. Check internet speed

### Messages Disappeared

1. Check browser storage isn't disabled
2. Try refreshing
3. Open in incognito/private mode
4. Contact support

### Getting Same Answer Every Time

1. Try rewording question
2. Ask for more details ("Tell me more...")
3. Ask different aspect of topic
4. Ask follow-up question
